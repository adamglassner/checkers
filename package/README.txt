CHECKERS - Hollow Creek General Store  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
====================================================

Open Checkers from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or the
Ironite Launcher. The game opens in its own window using Microsoft Edge
(built into Windows 10 and 11). Press F11 for full screen.

PLAY
- Play the computer: choose one of six regulars at the store, from
  Little Tucker (just learning) to Old Gus, the county champion. Play as
  Black (moves first), Red, or a random side.
- Two Players: pass and play on one computer. The board turns to face
  whoever is moving (you can switch that off in Options).
- Jumping is compulsory, as in tournament checkers. You can make it
  optional (house rules) on the title screen.

HOW TO MOVE
- Click a piece and then a square, or drag it. Dots show where it can go.
- Multi-jumps: click each landing square in turn, or just the last one.
  Press Esc to start a jump over.
- When you have to jump, the pieces that can glow gold.

KEYS
- Ctrl+Z undo, H hint, F flip board, N new game, M music on/off,
  Left/Right arrows step through the moves, Home/End jump to the start
  or the current position.

The score sheet uses the standard 1-32 square numbers (turn on Square
numbers in Options to see them on the board) and can copy the game as
text.

Your unfinished game, your record against each opponent and your
settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\Checkers, outside the install
folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.

All boards, pieces, portraits, music and sounds are original and made
by the game.
