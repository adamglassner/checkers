CHECKERS - Hollow Creek General Store  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
====================================================

Open Checkers from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or the
Ironite Launcher. Press F11 for full screen; the window can be resized.

PLAY
- Play the Computer: six regulars at the store are the six levels, from
  Little Tucker (just learning) to the county champion. They get a new
  name each time you sit down. Play as Black (moves first), Red, or a
  random side.
- Same Computer: two people share one PC, each with a name and a face.
  The board turns to face whoever is moving (switch that off in Options).
- Online: one player hosts and a friend joins with the host's address
  (TCP port 47020). Across the internet the host may need to forward
  that port on their router. The host picks the colours and the rules.
- Jumping is compulsory, as in tournament checkers. You can make it
  optional (house rules) on the title screen.

HOW TO MOVE
- Click a piece and then a square, or drag it. Dots show where it can go.
- Multi-jumps: click each landing square in turn, or just the last one.
  Press Esc (or right-click) to start a jump over.
- When you have to jump, the pieces that can glow gold.

KEYS
- Ctrl+Z undo, H hint, F flip board, N new game, M music on/off,
  Left/Right arrows step through the moves, Home/End jump to the start
  or the current position, F11 full screen.

The score sheet uses the standard 1-32 square numbers (turn on Square
numbers in Options to see them on the board) and can copy the game as
text.

Your unfinished game, your record and your settings are saved in
%APPDATA%\Ironite\Checkers, outside the install folder, so updating or
reinstalling keeps them.

All boards, pieces, portraits, music and sounds are original. The music
and sounds are played live by the game's own synthesizer.
